Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start my first round?

From the Home tab, tap "Start New Round." You'll be prompted to select or create a course, then you'll jump into the live round logger where you can score hole-by-hole. If you haven't added a course yet, tap "Add New Course" in the round setup.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device by default. We never collect or transmit your data to external servers. If you enable iCloud Sync in Settings, your data will additionally sync to your personal iCloud account.

How do I export my round data?

Go to Settings > Export CSV. This creates a comma-separated file with all your completed rounds including date, course, score, putts, fairway percentage, and GIR percentage. You can share this file via email, AirDrop, or save it to Files.

How is my handicap calculated?

TeeMetrics uses a simplified version of the USGA handicap formula. It calculates score differentials for your most recent 20 rounds, selects the best differentials (number varies by total rounds), averages them, and multiplies by 0.96. Please note this is an approximation — for an official handicap, register with your local golf association or GHIN.

What is Strokes Gained?

Strokes Gained is an advanced stat that compares your performance to a baseline in four categories: Off the Tee, Approach, Short Game, and Putting. Positive values mean you're gaining strokes (better than baseline), negative means you're losing strokes. TeeMetrics uses simplified PGA baselines for these calculations. Strokes Gained is available to Pro subscribers.

What's included in TeeMetrics Pro?

Pro unlocks: advanced analytics and trends (50+ rounds), strokes gained breakdown, club distance heatmaps, unlimited round history, CSV and PDF export, custom club sets, iCloud sync, and an ad-free experience. Available as monthly ($4.99), yearly ($29.99), or lifetime ($49.99).

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to iPhone Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > TeeMetrics > Cancel Subscription. Your Pro features will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the TeeMetrics purchase, and request a refund. Apple typically processes refund requests within 48 hours.

I lost my data. Can you recover it?

Because all data is stored locally on your device, we cannot recover data that has been deleted or lost due to device issues. We strongly recommend enabling iCloud Sync and periodically exporting your data as CSV to prevent data loss.

Does TeeMetrics work on Apple Watch?

Yes! TeeMetrics includes a companion Apple Watch app for quick score entry during rounds. You can log scores and putts for each hole directly from your wrist. The Watch app is available to all users (free and Pro).

How do I add a custom course?

When starting a new round, tap "Add New Course." Enter the course name, city, state, slope rating, and course rating. You can set up each hole's par and yardage individually, or use the "Standard Par 72 Layout" quick-fill button. You can also optionally pin the location on the map.

Troubleshooting

App is slow or freezing

Try force-quitting and reopening the app. If the issue persists, restart your device. TeeMetrics is designed to run smoothly with thousands of rounds, but very large datasets may benefit from periodic CSV export and data cleanup.

Scores not saving

TeeMetrics auto-saves as you score. If scores appear to be lost, ensure you haven't accidentally tapped "Delete All Data" in Settings. Check that your device has sufficient storage space.

iCloud Sync not working

Verify that iCloud is enabled in your device's Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud. Ensure TeeMetrics has iCloud access enabled. Try toggling iCloud Sync off and on in TeeMetrics Settings. Sync may take a few minutes depending on your connection.
