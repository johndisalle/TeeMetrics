Privacy Policy

TeeMetrics — Golf Stats Tracker & Round Analyzer

Last updated: April 7, 2026

TeeMetrics is designed with privacy at its core. Your golf data stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

1. Our Privacy Commitment

TeeMetrics is built as an offline-first, privacy-respecting application. We believe your golf statistics, scores, and personal data belong to you — not to advertisers, data brokers, or cloud services you didn't choose.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

TeeMetrics does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email, or contact information
  • Your location data or GPS coordinates
  • Your golf scores, statistics, or round history
  • Your device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data
  • Health or fitness data from HealthKit

3. Data Stored on Your Device

All data you enter into TeeMetrics is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This includes:

Data TypePurposeStorage
Profile (name, handicap)PersonalizationDevice only
Golf rounds & scoresRound trackingDevice only
Shot-by-shot dataPerformance analysisDevice only
Club & bag infoEquipment managementDevice only
Course dataCourse referenceDevice only
App preferencesSettingsDevice only

4. iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you enable iCloud Sync in Settings, your data will be stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. In this case:

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple
  • Data is stored in your iCloud account — we cannot access it
  • Syncing is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy
  • You can disable iCloud Sync at any time in TeeMetrics Settings

5. Apple Services Integration

5.1 MapKit

TeeMetrics uses Apple MapKit to display course maps. Map tile requests are sent directly from your device to Apple's servers. We do not receive or store your location data. Apple's use of this data is covered by their privacy policy.

5.2 HealthKit (Optional)

If you grant permission, TeeMetrics may read or write workout data to Apple Health. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us. You can revoke HealthKit permissions at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy > Health.

5.3 StoreKit

TeeMetrics Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive confirmation of purchase status but do not receive your payment details, Apple ID, or billing information.

6. Third-Party Services

TeeMetrics does not use:

  • Third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • Third-party advertising networks
  • Third-party crash reporting (no Crashlytics, Sentry, etc.)
  • Social media SDKs or tracking pixels
  • Any external backend servers

7. Data Export & Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Export: You can export all round data as CSV from Settings at any time
  • Delete: You can delete all data from Settings > Delete All Data
  • Uninstall: Deleting the app removes all locally stored data

8. Children's Privacy

TeeMetrics does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App does not require account creation or collect personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

9. Security

Your data is protected by iOS's built-in security features, including:

  • App sandboxing — no other app can access TeeMetrics data
  • Device encryption via your passcode/Face ID/Touch ID
  • iCloud encryption (if sync is enabled)

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.

11. Your Rights

Because TeeMetrics does not collect your data, most data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA) have minimal applicability. However, you always have the right to:

  • Access all data stored on your device (it's all in the app)
  • Export your data at any time
  • Delete your data at any time
  • Contact us with privacy concerns
