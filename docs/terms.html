2. Description of Service

TeeMetrics is an offline-first golf statistics tracking and round analysis application for iOS and Apple Watch. The App allows you to:

  • Log golf rounds hole-by-hole with quick-score and detailed scoring modes
  • Track shot-by-shot club performance and distances
  • View analytics including score trends, strokes gained, handicap projection, and round comparison
  • Set goals, earn achievements, and track weekly streaks
  • Manage your golf bag with 6 pre-built templates or custom configurations
  • Browse 660+ pre-loaded golf courses or share courses with the community via CloudKit

  • Log practice and range sessions
  • Use Siri Shortcuts for hands-free round management
